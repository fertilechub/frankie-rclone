Privacy Policy

Frankie rclone is a private backup utility used solely by its owner.

The application accesses Google Drive with read-only permissions in order to download and export files for personal backup purposes.

Google Drive data is stored only on storage systems controlled by the application owner and is not sold, shared, or provided to third parties.

The application does not provide services to the public and does not collect information from other users.
